# Premier Insurers

## Overview

**Pod:** Enrollment & Benefits, Policy Management
**Program:** Member Eligibility, Premium Collection

Premier Insurers manages a diverse portfolio of insurance products. Their data assets are critical for tracking policyholders, policy details, and managing member eligibility and premiums.

## Key Data Domains

*   **Policyholder Data:** Information about individuals holding insurance policies.
*   **Policy Data:** Details about each insurance policy, including coverage and premiums.
