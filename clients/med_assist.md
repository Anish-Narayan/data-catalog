# MedAssist

## Overview

**Pod:** Pharmacy Benefits Management, Drug Utilization Review
**Program:** Opioid Management, High-Cost Drug Monitoring

MedAssist specializes in pharmacy benefits management. Their data assets are critical for tracking prescription claims, drug utilization, and identifying potential fraud or abuse in pharmaceutical spending.

## Key Data Domains

*   **Pharmacy Claims:** Records of dispensed prescriptions.
*   **Drug Reference Data:** Information about prescription drugs and their classifications.
