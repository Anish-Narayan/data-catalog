# Acme Health

## Overview

**Pod:** Claims Analytics
**Program:** Duplicate Claims Detection, Payment Integrity Audits

Acme Health is a leading health insurance provider. This section details the data assets used for analyzing their claims, member, and provider information to identify potential payment integrity issues.

## Key Data Domains

*   **Claims Data:** Detailed records of services billed by providers.
*   **Member Data:** Demographic and enrollment information for insured individuals.
